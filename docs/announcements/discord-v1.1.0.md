**Premation 1.1.0 is out** 🎬

Our biggest update since 1.0. Highlights:

**✂️ Object Matte**: click a person or object to cut it out of your footage, track it through the shot, put text *behind* it, blur or replace the background, and fill the gap it leaves.

**📥 More imports**
• Photoshop files come in as a composition of their layers
• PDF and Illustrator files import as vector footage
• EXR stays float; JSON/CSV data can be read from expressions
• ProRes, DNx and MXF are no longer converted to 8-bit

**📤 Render Queue**: several outputs from one render, DNxHR / HEVC / AV1, TIFF and DPX sequences, post-render actions and templates.

**⚡ Faster playback**: frames are built on several cores, so heavy comps play about 2× faster before they're cached. Scrubbing responds instantly, and the viewer tells you when it's still rendering or dropping frames.

**🎨 Color and more**: Display P3 working space, HDR and camera-log footage, *Blend Colors Using 1.0 Gamma* as in After Effects, a keyframeable box behind text, and copy/paste/mirror for skeleton poses.

**🛠 Quality of life**
• Help ▸ **Report a Problem** sends us a screenshot and safe diagnostics. Your project is only included if you tick the box
• Checkout opens in your browser
• Check for Updates shows what's new
• Plugins can draw their own Effect Controls and dock their panels as tabs
